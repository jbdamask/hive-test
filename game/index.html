    How to play

    You are the queen. You lay the eggs; the bees that hatch work on their own. You decide what kind of bees the colony has.

    The colony mix gives each of the six castes a share of the eggs. Grow, Gather, Defend and Balanced set all six shares in one click, and plus and minus change one caste. The game starts on Grow. You can also pick the caste of the next egg by hand, and Match the mix now moves grown bees between castes to fit the mix.

    The castes. Builders turn honey into wax and build comb cells and new chambers. Nurses feed larvae and clean dirty cells. Foragers bring nectar and pollen from flower patches. Honeymakers turn nectar into honey. Scouts find new flower patches and spot hornets coming. Defenders guard the entrance and fight hornets.

    Eggs cost honey and pollen and need a clean, empty comb cell. A used cell is dirty until a nurse cleans it. If the queen stops laying, a notice says why and offers a fix.

    Murder hornets attack in waves, each larger than the last. When a scout sees a wave coming, the game pauses and you answer Defend or Keep my mix. Hornets that get past the defenders kill bees, eat young and take honey. If they reach the queen, the game ends.

    Points. At the end of the season you score your living bees, all the honey made, your comb cells and your chambers, added up.

    Keys. Space pauses and resumes. 1 is normal speed and 2 is fast speed. H opens and closes this page.
